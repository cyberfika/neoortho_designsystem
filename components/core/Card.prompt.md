Content container — 20px radius, hairline border, low neutral shadow.

```jsx
<Card surface="light"><h3>Excelência</h3><p>Excelência em produtos de qualidade.</p></Card>
<Card surface="onPurple">…</Card>
```

Use `onPurple` on the purple field; `muted` on the light slide field where a white card would disappear.
